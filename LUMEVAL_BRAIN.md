## 2. THE HONEST BUSINESS MODEL (as of June 2026)

### What Lumeval actually is today
A **physician services business** selling physician-signed MSK imaging-relevance verdicts to three B2B buyer types. The founder is the product. Revenue is per-verdict and per-engagement.

### What the Passport is (honest version)
A **byproduct feature** of the verdict business, not a separate acquisition problem. Every time Lumeval writes a verdict, the worker receives a Passport entry they own. No separate worker acquisition. No cold-start problem. The Passport rides on verdict volume.

### What it is NOT (yet)
- NOT a platform play requiring 500+ enrolled workers
- NOT a data trust licensing business (3–5 years out, if organic scale arrives)
- NOT a worker subscription business (5+ years out, if ever)
- NOT venture-scale today — it's a profitable physician services business with a differentiated brand and a slow-built moat

### The viable path for a solo physician founder
1. **Run the verdict business well.** Proof tests ($1,500), per-verdict ($500–1,500), backlog reviews ($20k+). This funds everything.
2. **Issue Passports as a byproduct.** Every verdict → worker gets a Passport entry. No separate acquisition. The moat compounds organically.
3. **If organic scale arrives (2,000+ Passports over 3 years):** revisit the platform/data-trust play.
4. **If it doesn't:** you still have a profitable physician services business with a differentiated brand.

### What was killed (honest)
- The worker-acquisition ambition (workers don't need to be acquired — they get Passports because you wrote them a verdict)
- The data trust ambition (distraction until organic scale)
- The platform play ambition (demoted to "byproduct feature" until scale says otherwise)

## 5. THE 8-CATEGORY STUCKNESS TAXONOMY (Lumeval IP)

Every long-aging MSK file has one of eight bottlenecks. Nobody else has published this. It's the category-defining IP.

| # | Category | Description | Verdict-eligible? | Avoidable days (low–high) |
|---|----------|-------------|-------------------|---------------------------|
| 1 | Imaging / report ambiguity | Imaging completed but report ambiguous on whether findings explain restrictions. No RTW decision made. | YES | 40–70 |
| 2 | Diagnostic ambiguity | Clinical picture unclear — conflicting signs, unclear mechanism, differential unresolved. | YES | 30–55 |
| 3 | Wrong / incomplete pathway | Case routed through wrong clinical pathway; specialist of wrong type consulted. | No | 25–45 |
| 4 | Specialist / escalation delay | Waiting for specialist consult that is months out in the public queue. | No | 45–90 |
| 5 | Rehab / function issue | Rehab stalled, function not progressing, no functional assessment completed. | No | 20–40 |
| 6 | Administrative handoff failure | Report completed but no RTW decision; lost between case manager, OH, supervisor. | No | 15–30 |
| 7 | Exposure protocol issue | Exposure incident protocol incomplete; causation window still open administratively. | No | 20–35 |
| 8 | Not imaging-related | Stuckness driver is non-imaging (psychosocial, systemic, legal-administrative). | No | 10–25 |

**Key insight:** Lumeval's verdict directly addresses categories 1–2 (~30% of stuck files). The other 6 require the accountability-map product (the backlog review), not the verdict.

## 6. THE VERDICT TEMPLATE (one-page structure)

```
VERDICT
File [ref] · [body part] · [modality] [time ago] · case age [days]

Question asked
[One operational question — usually: "Does the imaging finding explain the
current restrictions — yes or no?"]

Finding
[Plain-language description of the imaging finding, including how common/
asymptomatic it is in working-age adults]

Verdict
[Yes / No / Partial] — [one-sentence answer to the question asked]
[One-sentence clinical-pathway recommendation]

Recommended next step
[One concrete next action — e.g., "Duty-progression planning meeting between
OH team and supervisor; imaging does not need to be repeated"]

— Dr. Dan Gill, MD, FRCPC

STANDING LIMITATION (carried on every verdict):
This opinion provides imaging interpretation and clinical-pathway information
only. All return-to-work, duty, and claim decisions remain with the client's
responsible clinicians and decision-makers.
```

**Length:** One page. **SLA:** 5 business days. **Format:** Physician-signed, every time.

## 7. THE BOUNDARIES CHARTER (what this is NOT)

This is the single best defensibility argument. Productize it as a published artifact.

- **No IMEs.** No independent medical examinations.
- **No causation, compensability, or entitlement** determinations.
- **No return-to-work or fitness-for-duty** determinations.
- **No claim acceptance or denial.**
- **No employer access to individual worker medical information.** Employer-facing outputs are aggregate and de-identified with small-cell suppression.
- **No worker-level dashboards.** No credibility scoring. No fraud scoring.
- **No autonomous AI decisions.** AI assists with timeline extraction, classification, and drafting. A physician reviews and signs every verdict.
- **Litigated files excluded** from review. Appear in executive reporting as aggregate exposure visibility only.

**Tagline:** "Opinions inform decisions — they never make them."

## 10. REGULATORY CONSTRAINTS (Canada, by province)

### Federal
- **PIPEDA** — personal information protection. De-identified intake + small-cell suppression handles this.
- **Provincial physician licensure** — the binding constraint. A BC-licensed physician's opinion has reduced legal weight in AB/SK/ON/etc. until multi-province licensure is solved.

### By province (physician licensure for telemedicine opinions)
- **BC** — founder licensed. Full operation.
- **AB, SK, MB, ON, QC** — require provincial College licensure for opinions with legal weight. Clinical value travels; legal weight varies.
- **NWT/Yukon/Nunavut** — often covered by provincial license through arrangements; case-by-case.

### Indigenous data governance
- **OCAP® (Ownership, Control, Access, Possession)** — First Nations principles. Lumeval aligned by design (worker-owned, consent-governed).
- **Inuit Qaujimajatuqangit** and **Métis governance principles** — engagement-specific.
- **Indigenous-owned/co-managed operations** — engagement structures shaped WITH community governance, not around it.

### Workers'-comp board relationships (varies by province)
- **WorkSafeBC** (BC) — public insurer. Direct sales motion.
- **WCB Alberta, WCB Saskatchewan, WSIB (ON), CNESST (QC)** — public insurers, provincial scope.
- **Private insurers** (where applicable) — direct B2B sales.

## 12. THE BRUTAL TEAR-APART (investor lens)

### Three failure modes
1. **Cold-start worker acquisition** — both sides economically rational to decline the baseline transaction (employer weakens future dispute ability; worker risks limiting future claims). **Solved by:** employer-mandated baseline at hire with Passport as byproduct. Workers don't need to be acquired — they get Passports because you wrote them a verdict.
2. **Provincial licensure wall** — physician-signed verdict becomes legally inert across provincial borders. **Solved by:** multi-province licensure (in progress) OR accept reduced legal weight outside home province and sell the clinical value instead.
3. **Data trust revenue is a 5-year mirage** — AI vendors can get imaging+outcomes data cheaper elsewhere (OAI cohort, hospital PACS, radiology groups). **Solved by:** don't bet on this revenue stream. Run the verdict business. If organic scale arrives, revisit.

### Single load-bearing assumption
**Workers consent to create Passports BEFORE they have an injury.** If they only create Passports after injury, the baseline value prop collapses. **Solved by:** making the Passport a byproduct of the verdict business, not a separate enrollment decision.

### Investment verdict (honest, for a $10M cap)
**No today.** Three named failure modes, one load-bearing assumption, revenue 90% dependent on verdict sales in years 1–2. **What changes the verdict:**
1. 3 named employers mandating baseline at hire
2. 500+ workers enrolled (via verdict byproduct, not separate acquisition)
3. 2+ documented stuck-file-prevention cases with permission to reference
4. Provincial licensure plan or legal opinion on cross-border portability
5. One insurer LOI to pay per-access

**Honest assessment for solo founder:** Milestones 1–3 are hard but achievable over 18–24 months IF the verdict business generates organic Passport issuance. Don't pursue the platform play. Run the services business. Let the moat compound.
