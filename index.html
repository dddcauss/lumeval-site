Before the diagnostic pathway

Safety systems, training, standards, and controls help prevent incidents at the source.

Where Lumeval fits

Lumeval focuses on what happens after an injury, symptom report, referral, or abnormal imaging finding.

Why it matters

Better-coordinated imaging, travel, and follow-up can reduce repeat exams, worker uncertainty, and return-to-work disruption.

The next preventable failure point

In remote workforces, prevention does not end when the incident is controlled.

Strong site safety programs help prevent injuries before they happen. But after a workplace injury or symptom report, diagnostic pathways can still fail across imaging access, medical travel, occupational health, disability management, return-to-work, and operations.

30-Day Diagnostic Pathway Review

A focused first step to identify whether diagnostic imaging delays, repeat exams, medical travel, missed follow-up, or MSK pathway friction are creating preventable problems after injury or symptoms.

Imaging delays

Reviews where access to definitive imaging appears delayed.

Repeat or incomplete exams

Identifies repeated, incomplete, limited, or protocol-mismatched studies.

Medical travel

Maps diagnostic-related travel that may be better coordinated.

Missed follow-up

Flags abnormal findings or recommendations without clear closure where data is available.

MSK diagnosis and return-to-work

Reviews pathway friction linked to modified duty, absence, or unclear next steps.

What the review delivers

A practical post-incident prevention readout, not a data platform. The review is designed to produce a decision-ready summary without creating a new surveillance system or collecting individual health data for employer use.

  • 8-12 page aggregate diagnostic pathway report
  • simple map of where delays, repeat exams, missed follow-up, or unclear next steps occur
  • practical recommendations to improve diagnosis, treatment direction, travel coordination, and return-to-work flow
  • 60-minute readout with leadership or occupational health team
  • clear next step: no further work needed, targeted pathway improvement, broader diagnostic review, or ongoing monitoring

Intended outcome

The outcome Lumeval is built for

  • quicker diagnostic closure
  • fewer unnecessary repeat exams
  • better-coordinated medical travel
  • fewer missed follow-up steps
  • clearer treatment or specialist referral direction
  • less return-to-work uncertainty

Founder

Dr. Dan Gill, MD, FRCPC, is a Canadian musculoskeletal radiologist and founder of Lumeval. He has served as a Royal College examiner for 10 years and worked as an MSK radiology physician for the Vancouver 2010 Olympic Games.

Dr. Gill comes from a multi-generational British Columbia coal mining family and has first-hand experience working in open-pit mining environments. Lumeval combines subspecialty MSK imaging expertise with direct familiarity with remote industrial workforces.
