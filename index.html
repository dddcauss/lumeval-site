THE PATHWAY

Lumeval reviews the diagnostic interval.

Lumeval provides specialist MSK radiology review and operational analysis of the interval between injury, imaging access, diagnosis, follow-up, and return-to-work coordination.

  1. 01Specialist review: FRCPC MSK radiology review of existing imaging and diagnostic records to identify clinically relevant gaps, repeat imaging patterns, and unresolved MSK questions.
  2. 02Operational assessment: Diagnostic interval analysis across imaging access, medical travel, follow-up, modified duty, and return-to-work coordination to identify where friction accumulates.

THE THIRTY-DAY REVIEW

Six measurements. One readout.

We measure where time and cost accumulate in your diagnostic pathway — and where they can be recovered.

01

Time to imaging

How long an injured worker waits to reach definitive imaging — and where the time accumulates.

02

Repeat imaging

Scans that get redone, run incompletely, or use the wrong protocol. What they cost.

03

Medical travel

Diagnostic travel episodes. Which were necessary. Which could have been coordinated better.

04

Follow-up closure

Abnormal findings without documented follow-up. Where they sit in your operation.

05

MSK friction

Musculoskeletal cases on modified duty. Where the diagnostic pathway is slowing return-to-work.

06

Aggregate readout

Your operation's diagnostic pathway at the site level. Aggregate only. No individual data.
