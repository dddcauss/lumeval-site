THE GAP

Occupational health cases do not only get delayed. They become unowned.

In heavy industry, unresolved cases often drift between site operations, occupational health, claims, HR, insurers, rehab, specialists, supervisors, and RTW processes. Lumeval creates decision accountability by identifying the missing decision, assigning the accountable owner, setting a deadline, defining an escalation path, and tracking follow-up.

CASE BUCKETS

Stuck does not mean one thing.

Cases are categorized by the dominant reason they are not moving.

  • Diagnostic ambiguity
  • Imaging/report ambiguity
  • Wrong or incomplete pathway
  • Specialist/escalation delay
  • Rehab/function issue
  • Administrative handoff failure
  • Report completed but no RTW decision
  • Exposure protocol issue
  • Not imaging-related

FAQ

Direct answers to the questions operating teams ask.

A worker on one of our stuck cases has a lawyer. Can you review it?

No. Represented or litigated files are excluded from case review. They appear in executive reporting only as aggregate exposure visibility. This protects the worker, your legal position, and the integrity of the pilot metrics.

What will our union think of this?

The model is built to be worker-positive: no claims adjudication, no IMEs, no fitness-for-duty assessments, no credibility or fraud scoring, no individual worker dashboards, and no employer access to individual worker medical information. The purpose is that no worker is left in limbo because the next step is unclear. We encourage clients to brief worker representatives before the pilot starts and can support that conversation.

How is worker privacy protected?

The pilot starts with a de-identified case list — no names, birthdates, addresses, SINs, or health card numbers. Documents are requested only for the 30 selected cases and transferred through a secure workflow arranged directly with your team, never by email. Employer-facing outputs are aggregate and de-identified with small-cell suppression. Full details are in our Security & Data Handling overview (PDF download).

What happens after the map is delivered?

The map is the start, not the deliverable. The pilot includes two structured follow-up reviews — day 14 and day 30 — with your named internal sponsor, confirming each action moved, escalating where it did not, and measuring avoidable days. Accountability without follow-through is a PDF in a drawer; the follow-up cadence is the product.

Is this an IME or a medical-legal opinion?

No. Lumeval does not determine causation, compensability, entitlement, disability, impairment, or fitness for duty, and is not a claim acceptance or denial tool. It is an operational review layer that clarifies the missing decision, the owner, the deadline, and the escalation path inside your existing occupational health and RTW processes.

How is AI used?

AI assists with timeline extraction, classification, and drafting. A physician reviews and finalizes every accountability map. AI makes no autonomous claim, entitlement, fitness-for-duty, or medical-legal decisions.

What does the pilot require from our team?

Three things: one named internal sponsor empowered to assign owners, the de-identified case export within two weeks of signing, and attendance at the two follow-up reviews.
