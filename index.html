THE PATHWAY

Lumeval works in the interval no one audits.

Site safety, occupational health, claims administration, and disability management each operate at the bookends of the post-injury pathway. The diagnostic interval between — imaging, travel, diagnosis, and follow-up — fragments across them all.

  1. 01Pre-injury — Site safety
  2. 02First response — Occupational health
  3. 03Imaging & travel — Lumeval
  4. 04Diagnosis & follow-up — Lumeval

THE THIRTY-DAY REVIEW

Six measurements. One readout.

We measure where time and cost accumulate in your diagnostic pathway — and where they can be recovered.

01

Time to imaging

How long an injured worker waits to reach definitive imaging — and where the time accumulates.

02

Repeat imaging

Scans that get redone, run incompletely, or use the wrong protocol. What they cost.

03

Medical travel

Diagnostic travel episodes. Which were necessary. Which could have been coordinated better.

04

Follow-up closure

Abnormal findings without documented follow-up. Where they sit in your operation.

05

MSK friction

Musculoskeletal cases on modified duty. Where the diagnostic pathway is slowing return-to-work.

06

Aggregate readout

Your operation's diagnostic pathway at the site level. Aggregate only. No individual data.

Deliverables

A practical, decision-ready readout.

Designed to produce a decision-ready summary without creating a new surveillance system or collecting individual health data for employer use.

  • Eight-to-twelve-page aggregate diagnostic pathway report
  • Operational map of where delay, repeat exams, missed follow-up, or unclear next steps occur
  • Practical recommendations to improve diagnosis, treatment direction, travel coordination, and return-to-work flow
  • Sixty-minute readout with leadership or the occupational health team
  • A clear recommended next step — no further work, targeted pathway improvement, broader diagnostic review, or ongoing monitoring

Privacy and worker-trust framework

Aggregate. Clinically governed. Voluntary.

Lumeval is prevention- and pathway-focused. Employer-facing outputs are aggregate and de-identified. Individual clinical information remains clinically governed.

Employer reporting boundaries

  • Aggregate-only employer reporting
  • No individual worker dashboard
  • Small-cell suppression
  • No employer access to individual worker medical information
  • Individual clinical information remains clinically governed
  • Indigenous data-governance pathways where applicable

Not part of the service

  • No claims adjudication
  • No independent medical examination
  • No fitness-for-duty assessment
  • No routine screening
  • No legal causation opinions
  • No employer access to individual worker medical information

Risk, occupational health, safety, and operations leadership.
