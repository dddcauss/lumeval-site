Practice

The next preventable failure point

Prevention does not end when the incident is controlled.

Strong site safety programs prevent injuries before they happen. After a workplace injury or symptom report, diagnostic pathways still fail across imaging access, medical travel, occupational health, disability management, return-to-work, and operations.

  • Delayed access to MRI and CT
  • Repeat or incomplete imaging
  • Avoidable medical travel
  • Abnormal-finding follow-up gaps
  • Unclear musculoskeletal diagnosis
  • Return-to-work uncertainty

The gap

The diagnostic pathway has no audit owner.

Occupational medicine, disability management, claims administration, independent medical examinations, workers' compensation, and EHS platforms each address parts of the post-injury workflow. None step back and review the diagnostic pathway between injury and definitive diagnosis as an operational category.

Imaging access timing. Repeat exam patterns. Medical travel coordination. Abnormal-finding follow-up. Time-to-treatment direction. These accumulate across functions that do not share data, and across vendor categories whose mandates do not include this work.

Lumeval was built to close that gap — subspecialty musculoskeletal imaging expertise, operational familiarity with remote workforces, and a clinically governed, worker-centred framework that produces aggregate operational findings without creating individual surveillance.

The thirty-day review

30-Day Diagnostic Pathway Review

A thirty-day review designed to identify whether diagnostic imaging delays, repeat exams, medical travel, missed follow-up, or musculoskeletal pathway friction are creating preventable cost and care delay at your operation.

01

Imaging access and delay

Reviews where time from injury or symptom report to definitive imaging accumulates at the operational level.

02

Repeat or incomplete imaging

Identifies repeated, incomplete, technically limited, or protocol-mismatched studies where data is available.

03

Medical travel and coordination

Maps diagnostic-related travel episodes that may be avoidable, compressible, or better coordinated.

04

Abnormal-finding follow-up

Flags imaging recommendations or abnormal findings without documented closure where data is available.

05

Musculoskeletal pathway friction

Reviews aggregate pathway points contributing to prolonged modified duty, absence, or return-to-work uncertainty.
