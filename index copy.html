THE GAP

Occupational health cases do not only get delayed. They become unowned.

In heavy industry, unresolved cases often drift between site operations, occupational health, claims, HR, insurers, rehab, specialists, supervisors, and RTW processes. Lumeval creates decision accountability by identifying the missing decision, assigning the accountable owner, setting a deadline, defining an escalation path, and tracking follow-up.

CASE BUCKETS

Stuck does not mean one thing.

Cases are categorized by the dominant reason they are not moving, including imaging or report ambiguity when that is the true blocker.

  • Diagnostic ambiguity
  • Imaging/report ambiguity
  • Wrong or incomplete pathway
  • Specialist/escalation delay
  • Rehab/function issue
  • Administrative handoff failure
  • Report completed but no RTW decision
  • Exposure protocol issue
  • Not imaging-related

PROOF METRICS

Measured by movement, not opinion.

The pilot does not lead with claimed savings. It measures whether stuck cases move from indeterminate to next-step-defined.

  • Cases with a clearly identified missing decision
  • Cases assigned to a named owner
  • Cases assigned a deadline and escalation path
  • Cases with documented follow-up status
  • Cases moved from indeterminate to next-step-defined
  • Repeat bottlenecks by site, function, or provider
  • Cases requiring escalation
  • Cases removed from the wrong pathway

OPERATIONAL EXPOSURE

Operational exposure, not guaranteed savings.

Lumeval estimates operational exposure using employer-supplied assumptions. In mining and heavy industry, unresolved cases can involve replacement labour, modified-duty aging, medevac/offsite removal, camp logistics, supervisor time, delayed maintenance, and production-risk exposure.

  • Estimated operational exposure
  • Employer-supplied assumptions
  • Risk-weighted
  • Not guaranteed savings
  • Actual value depends on case mix, implementation, and whether actions are completed

WORKER-POSITIVE

No worker should be left in limbo because the next step is unclear.

The pilot is designed to improve process clarity, reduce unresolved case drift, and support timely coordination. It does not assess claim validity, worker credibility, entitlement, compensability, disability, impairment, or fitness for duty.

BOUNDARIES

What this is not.

Lumeval does not perform IMEs, determine causation, compensability, entitlement, disability, impairment, or fitness for duty. Lumeval is not a claim acceptance or denial tool and does not replace occupational health. Lumeval supports existing occupational health and RTW processes by clarifying unresolved decisions, owners, deadlines, escalation paths, and follow-up status.

REVIEW MODEL

AI-assisted. Physician-led.

Lumeval uses AI-assisted timeline extraction and classification to organize de-identified case materials. A physician reviews and finalizes the accountability maps. AI does not make autonomous claim, entitlement, fitness-for-duty, or medical-legal decisions.

DATA WORKFLOW

Start with a de-identified case export.

To reduce work on the client side, Lumeval starts with a de-identified export of open physical injury and exposure cases older than 90 days, with 180+ day cases flagged. Lumeval selects 30 high-friction cases for the pilot and then requests limited documents only for those selected cases.

  • De-identified case ID
  • Injury/exposure date
  • Case age in days
  • Job role/trade
  • Body part or exposure type
  • Current status
  • Days offsite if available
  • Days modified duty if available
  • Last occupational health letter date
  • Last action date
  • Next pending action if known
  • Imaging/test performed yes/no
  • Specialist referral pending yes/no
  • Rehab/PT active yes/no
  • Medevac/offsite removal yes/no
  • Reason case appears stuck, if known

No worker names, SINs, health card numbers, dates of birth, addresses, or unnecessary personal identifiers are required for the initial case list. No public medical-record upload is available on this website. Pilot data transfer is arranged directly with the client using a secure workflow.
